The problem

  • Hardware startups die in ways software startups don't: single-source parts, a BOM too high for the price, tooling costs, missing certifications
  • Spotting them needs engineering, supply chain, regulatory, and finance judgment, from a deck and a few spreadsheets
  • Investors see more hardware deals than specialist time. First-pass screens are slow and inconsistent

Every risk must cite an exact quote. A verifier drops any that isn't in the documents.

How the agents work

7 LLM agents plus code for ingest, verification, and scoring. The documents are written by the startup, so they are untrusted.
